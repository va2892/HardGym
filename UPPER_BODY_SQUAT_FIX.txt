HardGym v0.10.5 — squat upper-body/bar alignment

Changed from v0.10.4:
- Legs keep exactly the v0.10.4 ground-contact positions.
- Standing phase (ANIM 0): unchanged.
- Middle phase (ANIM 1): torso/head/arms raised 3.0 model pixels.
- Bottom phase (ANIM 2): torso/head/arms raised 4.6 model pixels.
- Mounted bar and all plates are raised by the exact same amount in ANIM 1/2.
- No whole-player MatrixStack lift is added; the correction is model-part-only.

Reason:
v0.10.4 raised the leg roots but left the torso hip anchor at the old height,
so the lower torso could visibly protrude into the thighs. v0.10.5 aligns the
upper-body hip anchor with the raised leg roots while leaving the legs fixed.
